Built on Nova Engine 5

Engineered for speed

Every system in Velocity X exists for one reason: to make 400 km/h feel dangerous again.

Micro-Slip Physics

A 12-point contact patch model simulates tyre temperature, camber and rubber deposits in real time. Drifts build heat, heat changes grip, grip changes everything.

  • 240 Hz solver
  • Tyre wear

Ray-Traced Neon

Hardware ray tracing resolves every sign, puddle and chrome panel. Wet asphalt becomes a mirror of the skyline at 4K with DLSS 4 and FSR 4 frame generation.

  • RT reflections
  • Path-traced GI

Adaptive Rivals

Opponent drivers learn your braking points, your favourite lines and your weaknesses, then trade paint to exploit them. No rubber-banding, ever.

  • Neural AI
  • Zero catch-up

Deep Garage

Swap turbos, gear ratios, diff locks and aero, then paint it with a 200-layer livery editor. 1.4 million community liveries and counting.

  • Livery editor
  • Full tuning

Reactive Soundtrack

An adaptive score written with 40 artists reshapes itself around your throttle. Convolution-modelled exhausts recorded from the real cars at Nardò.

  • Dolby Atmos
  • Live stems

Seamless Seasons

A 90-day live cycle adds districts, weather systems and story chapters with zero re-downloads thanks to streaming delta patches under 400 MB.

  • Live events

    Support

    Frequently asked

    Still stuck? The crew answers tickets in under four hours.

    Is Velocity X free to play?

    Yes. The Standard Edition — full campaign prologue, 18 cars, every online mode and the complete garage editor — is free forever with no time limit. Paid editions unlock the 120-car roster, the full story and seasonal cosmetics.

    Does it support cross-play and cross-progression?

    Cross-play is on by default across PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and Switch 2, and can be disabled per lobby. A free Nova ID syncs your garage, liveries, career progress and battle pass tiers between every device, including mobile.

    Can I play offline?

    The 30-hour Night Circuit campaign, Time Attack, Free Roam and the full garage work completely offline. Ranked ladders, convoys and live events need a connection. Offline earnings sync the next time you connect.

    Which wheels and controllers are supported?

    Over 40 wheel bases from Logitech, Thrustmaster, Fanatec, Moza and Simucube are supported natively with individually tuned force-feedback profiles, plus load-cell pedals and H-pattern shifters. On console we support DualSense adaptive triggers and Xbox impulse triggers.

    Are there loot boxes or pay-to-win mechanics?

    None. There are no randomised paid rewards anywhere in the game, and nothing purchasable changes vehicle performance. The store sells cosmetics and optional progression boosts only, and every car in it can be earned by racing.

    Is there mod support?

    Velocity Forge ships free on PC with the Ultimate Edition and lets you author liveries, tracks, vehicle physics and full campaign chapters. Approved mods appear in a curated in-game workshop, and creators keep 70% of any revenue from premium packs.

    What accessibility options are available?

    Full remapping, one-button driving, adjustable assist granularity, colour-blind presets with per-channel tuning, 60–200% UI scaling, screen-reader menus, subtitle backing plates, camera-shake and motion-blur toggles, and audio cue substitutes for every visual alert.

    When is the next season?

    Seasons run on a fixed 90-day cadence. Season 05 “Chrome Requiem” opens the harbour district, adds night storms and 14 new cars. Ultimate owners get access 72 hours early.
